- When you keep shooting during a backup, and new photos are the only thing a drive doesn't have yet, the backup now finishes calmly: "Backup complete. New files will be included in the next backup." No orange warning or failure sound. The drive still isn't called identical until the next backup copies them. Anything else that changed (an edited, renamed or removed file, a Capture One session database that changed, something unreadable, a name the drive can't store) still asks you to look.
- A Backup Check after LiveSync no longer reads every photo LiveSync already copied and checked again in full, so it's much quicker. A copy that changed on either side since (even keeping its size and date) is still read and compared.
- Still One-Way Backup only, as in 0.0.9: Multi-Way Sync stays paused, and files that arrive during a backup wait for the next one.
